FAQ

Common questions.

How much does it cost?+
Glad you asked. The consult is free — and we have yet to have a customer whose bill we didn't cut in half. Always different. Always fair. Always custom built to be exactly what you want, even if you don't quite know what that means yet. This is uncharted territory. That's what the consult is for.
My business is too small for AI. Is it?+
Probably not. If you have repeatable processes, customer interactions, or data you're not using — there's an AI application. We've built solutions for 3-person shops. Size doesn't matter. Process does.
How long does a typical project take?+
Strategy: 1-2 weeks. A simple automation: 2-4 weeks. Custom AI tool: 4-12 weeks. We work in sprints and you see progress weekly. Nothing disappears into a black box for months.
Do I need to understand AI to work with you?+
No. That's literally why we exist. You know your business — we know AI. You describe the problem, we figure out the solution and explain it in plain language. Every time.
What industries do you work with?+
Construction, agriculture, oil & gas, healthcare, legal, real estate, retail, professional services, manufacturing, logistics. If your business operates in North Dakota, we can probably help.
What happens after launch?+
We don't ghost you. Every project includes 30 days of post-launch support. After that, you can opt into managed services where we monitor, optimize, and maintain your AI systems ongoing.
