Common questions

Straight answers.

How much does it cost?

The first consultation is free. Pricing depends on the work: an audit, training, automation, or a custom build with ongoing support. We review your current tools and costs, then give you a scope and price to consider before you commit.

Is my business too small for AI?

If you have repetitive work, scheduling, paperwork, or follow-up that eats hours every week — you're exactly the right size. The businesses seeing the biggest returns aren't the giant ones; they're the ones where saving ten hours a week actually matters.

Do I need to understand AI to work with you?

No. That's the point of us. We explain everything in plain English, train your team in person, and never hide behind jargon. You understand your business; we'll handle the technology.

How long does a typical project take?

Audits and strategy work land in weeks, not months. Builds depend on scope — but you see progress every week, tested against your real data, and we show up with a working demo before you've committed to anything.

What happens after launch?

We don't disappear. Deployment, training, monitoring, and ongoing support are covered in your agreed subscription scope. We work through support needs and responsibilities with you before launch, so you know who to call and what is included.

What industries do you work with?

Construction, trades, retail, sports organizations, professional services — operators, mostly. If your business runs on real-world work and the software is the bottleneck, we speak your language.
